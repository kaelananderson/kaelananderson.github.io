Hi! I am someone who is curious about the questions that live at the intersection of data and people. Healthcare quality and equity, the social determinants of health, racial inequity, the sociology of homelessness and houselessness, sustainability, public health. I like intersectional frameworks, and I am always looking for the more complete picture of why things are the way they are.

Right now I am putting that curiosity to work in two places. At Kaiser Permanente, I build dashboards and reporting tools for the Maui Health System, and that work has taught me a lot about how good data in the right hands at the right time actually changes the care people receive. I also do research at San Diego State University's Geospatial Human Dynamics Lab, where I use spatial statistics to find significant space-time clusters in 311 calls about homeless encampments, trying to understand where and when these issues concentrate and what that might tell us. I have also worked on projects spanning comparative machine learning models for high-dimensional data and basketball performance analytics.

I am energized by environments that value creativity, technical rigor, and a commitment to social justice. I am inspired by work that helps organizations understand their data in ways that lead to better outcomes for the people they serve.

Outside of work and research, I coach youth basketball, write poetry, and if you can not find me at my computer or in the gym, I am probably watching the sunset.
