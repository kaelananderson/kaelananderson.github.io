Why I Am Not a Poet

After Frank O'Hara's "Why I Am Not a Painter"

I am not a poet, I am a mimic. Why? I think I could be a poet, but I am not. Well, for instance, I fail to write poems without I. And when I try, it is only a copy of a poem I have read. I read. I write. I am a mimic. Can't you see? All the I's that converge to the page, even when I attempt to be intentional. Riley and I sit to write, poems, you could call them. We write. We share. He writes in French, and it becomes poetic without meaning to the ears. But me? One day I am thinking of a tree. I write a line about a tree. It starts with I. Pretty soon it is a page full of I's. And trees. It could be less self-centered, it could have more empathy. Is it even a “poem” if all I do is treat it like a journal with metaphors to hide behind? It is finished now, this one too, and all that I could write about was I.
