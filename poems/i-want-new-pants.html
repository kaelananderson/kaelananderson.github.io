I Want New Pants

There is a new pair of pants that I want. Nice, comfortable and I love the way they hover right over my shoes. They are my new thing to desire. When I find the right ones, I'll let you know. I'll be safe. O, but what I really want is something too radical I don't feel safe giving it a whisper. For I fear I may lose your love and that my truest self will make you uncomfortable. Because that is not what you desire of my life, right?
