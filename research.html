NSF REU · UC Riverside · Summer 2024

Interpretable Modeling for High-Dimensional Data: Comparing Lasso Regression and Neural Networks

Compared Lasso regression against CNNs and FCNNs on four high-dimensional datasets — synthetic simulation data, wave energy farm output, TCGA stomach cancer genomics, and Taiwanese bankruptcy records. Evaluated accuracy, efficiency, feature selection, and interpretability to identify when a simpler, transparent model can match or outperform deep learning. Supported by NSF Grant No. 2244480 through UC Riverside's "Experience the Full Data Science Pipeline" REU site.
